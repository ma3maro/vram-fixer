# Changelog

## 1.1.0

First public release.

- Camera/reference-aware image and mesh budgets, protected-object list, preview and persistent Restore.
- Nested shader groups, group-instance coordinate routing, ramps/mixes and active-output image discovery.
- Coverage-weighted UV demand and 256-pixel texture resolution steps.
- Current-frame transform animation support and fewer blanket material exclusions.
- Sampled geometric-error validation with Decimate-ratio backoff.
- Policy and actual-Blender regression fixtures, original burning-room demo, real memory-pressure results and visual release kit.

Known host-RAM limitations and cases deliberately skipped are documented. No guaranteed hard-OOM rescue or universal render-speed improvement is claimed.
