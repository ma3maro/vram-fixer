# Asset and dependency notes

- Add-on code: GPL-3.0-or-later, with the full license in `LICENSE` and `vram_fixer/LICENSE`.
- Burning-room geometry and texture bakes were authored for this project. No supplied comic image is embedded or traced. The sleepy dog and room are native editable meshes/curves/materials. Familiar burning-room concepts, optional quotes and related marks are not represented as rights granted by this repository; review rights before a meme-specific campaign.
- Avocado pressure-test media are final renders from the author's scene. Its source `.blend` and third-party texture/material libraries are not distributed. The stress-test settings and numerical results are public separately.
- Poster/animation fonts: Big Shoulders, Bricolage Grotesque and DM Mono. Their OFL notices are included next to the font files in `promo/assets` and `promo-pressure/assets`.
- The before/after reveal is adapted from HyperFrames' `before-after-wipe` component. The installed reference file is retained in `promo/compositions/components`.
- Editable motion projects use HyperFrames and GSAP. GSAP's distributed file retains its copyright/license header; its package README/license link is included in `promo/assets/GSAP-NOTICE.md`. Those dependency terms are separate from the add-on's GPL license.

The preview images are reduced-size exports of real renders. Neither comparison side was selectively desaturated, blurred or retouched to exaggerate the improvement.
