# Controlled burn

The picture carries the claim. Preserve the warmth, material detail and quiet absurdity of the rendered room. Set it against deep green-black space so the flames feel bright without changing either comparison image. The artwork should look meticulously crafted, not buried beneath promotional chrome.

Typography is structural. A condensed headline supplies weight; a restrained geometric face names the product; a mono face labels measurements. Use warm ivory, ember orange and one pale electric-green accent. Numbers stay attached to their precise measurement. Painstaking alignment and generous margins matter more than extra ornament.

Before and after occupy the same coordinates. The reveal moves; the camera, exposure and pictures do not. No desaturation, blur or fake damage is applied to the original to make the optimized image look better. This is master-level execution through restraint: the visual evidence should survive close inspection.

Treat the two tests as two separate exhibits. The original burning-room artwork illustrates camera-aware optimization. The avocado pressure test illustrates a measured allocation above an 8 GB card's dedicated capacity, reduced while retaining the shot. Their labels, figures and settings never migrate between cases.

End with access, not scarcity. Free software, visible source, downloadable scenes and readable evidence are the offer. A strong finish is a useful GitHub link, not a countdown, invented crash dialog or unsupported promise. The product of deep expertise should be a clear invitation to inspect the work.
